The Genesis Fellowship

The Merit & Equity Allocation

"True architectural sovereignty is built on raw execution, not financial background. We ensure that the sharpest minds have a seat at the Forge."

The Krishnaite Academy operates as a strict meritocracy. We recognize that some of the most capable, visionary engineers emerge from environments where access to high-tier capital is constrained. To ensure these elite minds are not gated out of our ecosystem, we have established the Genesis Fellowship. This is not standard financial aid; it is a highly competitive grant reserved strictly for the top percentile of academic performers.

The Fellowship Allocation

Candidates who successfully pass the rigorous application gate will be awarded a 20% to 25% direct subsidy on their core annual tuition for either the Sovereign Forge (4-Year) or the Vanguard Launchpad (2-Year) track.

The Eligibility Gate

Candidates must simultaneously meet both of the following uncompromising criteria:

  • 1. Elite Academic Proof (The 93% Standard)

    You must possess an absolute minimum of 93% aggregate in your most recent academic grading cycle (12th-grade board examinations or recent university semesters). We demand proof of extreme discipline and traditional academic dominance before transitioning you to our execution-based model.

  • 2. The Financial Threshold

    You must provide legal documentation proving a total annual family income of strictly less than ₹3,00,000.

Note: Meeting these minimum thresholds qualifies you for review but does not guarantee an allocation. The final 4 seats are awarded based on absolute rank.

The Application Protocol

If you meet the structural requirements above, you must prepare the following documentation to initiate your Fellowship claim:

  • Official Academic Transcripts

    Verified mark sheets proving your 93%+ standing in your last completed cycle.

  • Government-Issued Income Certificate

    Valid and current legal documentation verifying your family's annual income is strictly below the ₹3 Lakh threshold.

  • The Unstop Entrance Exam Benchmark

    You must first take and achieve a top-tier rank in our official Logic & Systems Thinking Entrance Exam.

Terms & Conditions of Sovereignty

  • Maintenance of Merit

    The 20% to 25% subsidy is maintained year-over-year strictly on the condition that the candidate maintains peak performance within the Academy, ships their required digital platforms on time, and upholds the highest standards of our code of conduct.

  • Verification Standard

    Any falsification of academic records or financial documentation will result in immediate, non-negotiable, and permanent expulsion from the Krishnaite Academy network.
