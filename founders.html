Meet the Founders

"We built Krishnaite because traditional education teaches you to read manuals, while the market pays you to build systems. We are technical operators training the next wave of sovereign software builders."

Parents invest in people, not logos. At Krishnaite Global Academy, we run a strictly founder-led environment. We do not delegate lectures to assistants or buy off-the-shelf pre-recorded courses. We design, lecture, code, and review applications alongside our candidates every single week.

Harshita Bhaskaruni

Founder & CEO of Mentozy & Krishnaite

Harshita Bhaskaruni is a tech entrepreneur, software engineer, and full-stack developer. Originally born and raised in Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh, she attended Ravindra Bharati Public School and completed higher secondary education at Sri Sarada Educational Institutions. She is currently a Computer Science undergraduate student at the Birla Institute of Technology and Science (BITS), Pilani.

Mentozy & Krishnaite Ecosystem: In early 2026, Harshita officially launched Mentozy, an all-in-one edtech ecosystem built on the premise that "anyone can teach, and anyone can learn." The platform operates as a peer-driven learning and mentorship network bypassing financial and geographic limitations. As CEO, Harshita manages core product development and a global network of builders.

Technical Expertise & AI Innovation: Proficient in Python, Java, HTML, JavaScript, and 3D modeling, she has led multiple AI-driven applications:

  • INGRES-AI: Project Manager for this platform, simplifying complex government data portals to offer farmers, researchers, and policymakers actionable insights on groundwater conservation.
  • HoneyGPT & NoteBee: Developed productivity tools designed to streamline note-taking and real-time editing.

Recognition: Honored as a Youth Icon of India for her commitments to early-stage software innovation and accessible education, she actively contributes to NGOs and youth development programs across Andhra Pradesh.
