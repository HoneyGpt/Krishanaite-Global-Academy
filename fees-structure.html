The Admission Gateway & Transaction Infrastructure

Tier-One Compliant Capital Deployment

To ensure a seamless and secure financial commitment, all transactions are processed through our compliant, tier-one digital gateway structure.

1. The Mandatory Admission Retainer

Upon successfully passing the automated Logic Entrance Exam and receiving an official acceptance letter from Krishnaite, a mandatory ₹50,000 Admission Fee is required to lock in your seat in the Genesis Cohort. This acts as your official onboarding commitment and activates your digital campus access.

2. Flexible Capital Deployment (Payment Modes)

Once the admission retainer is secured, the remaining semester balances can be cleared via two authorized methods:

  • Upfront Semester Clearance: Full payment of the semester block (e.g., ₹1,00,000) at the commencement of every 4-month cycle.
  • Monthly EMI Protocols: Authorized month-to-month automatic financing via our approved payment dashboard, allowing you to split the semester block into manageable 30-day installments.
